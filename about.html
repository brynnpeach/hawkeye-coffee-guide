About this student project

A guide designed around student needs.

Hawkeye Coffee Guide is a fictional website created as an educational project for MKTG:4400 at the University of Iowa. It explores how useful content, clear navigation, and search-focused page titles can help a website serve a specific audience.

Who is this guide for?

University of Iowa students who want to compare coffee-shop-style environments for breakfast, independent study, or group work.

What information does it cover?

The example pages consider study atmosphere, seating, sample menus, illustrative prices, and imagined amenities. These details are invented and should not be used to make real-world purchasing or travel decisions.

Why is the site fictional?

All business names, people, descriptions, prices, and features were generated for this class project. The site is not affiliated with any real café or the University of Iowa.
